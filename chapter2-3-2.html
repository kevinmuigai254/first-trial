CHAPTER TWO

Benson was peacefully sleeping in the middle of the night.Benson was still experiencing home sickness since when Benson was at home he really liked girls and attending parties so on this fine night as he was sleeping he started dreaming.He was dreaming that he was in party.A fine lady came to where he was and gave him a glass of wine.Benson began drinking the wine.Just then benson realised that the wine was salty.That was when benson woke up and found out that he was actually not in a party but he was at school.To his surprise, the guy sleeping at the upper bed had wet the bed and the urine had gone down to his mouth as he was sleeping below him.Benson woke up as a very angry man he wanted to beat up his bedmate,but he couldn't since his bedmate was not only the school captain but also the only son of the school principal hence he was untouchable.Well this scenario kept on repeating itself untill one day benson felt that he had experienced enough and on that day he decided to take his chances so he went to report his bedmate to the principal.When he went to the principal's office he found the office door open but he was not inside.He decided to wait for him inside his office.Just then the school chef brought the principal's food and he left.Benson was left alone in the office with the principal's food on the table.Well as the rest of the school ate rice and beans every day the principal had his own special meal which was prepared separately by the school chef.The principal's meal had chicken,salad with extra beef.Benson was salivating at the principal's office.He was in a dilemma wondering whether he should take a bite from the principal's food or not.He tried resisting but he could not hold it anymore so he took a piece of the meat.Before he could put the piece of meat into his mouth he heard footsteps approaching.He panicked and since he had no time to return it to the plate and he could not risk swallowing a huge chank of meat he hid the meat in his pocket.Just then the principal entered the office.Well the meat that he kept in his pocket had soup so the soup trickled down to his shoes.Well that was the first thing that the principal noticed when he stepped into the office.Benson had to pretend that he had urinated on himself since he could not tell the truth.The principal gave him a hot slap and he asked him to go and change.Well the slap was not even the issue.When he kept the meat in his pocket the meat was still hot so all that time he was in pain as the meat was burning his pocket.Well despite all this he went and changed and he came back to the principal and he narrated his problems and he was assigned a new bedmate.Benson knew very well that he had to work hard in school to get the grades since people had contributed their money for his studies and they all had high hopes in him.By the time he knew it four years were over and he was now in the final grade of highschool.This was the most important grade since at the end of the year he would sit down for the national exams that would determine whether he would join the university.Finally it was the end of the year and it was now time for the national exam.Benson was so anxious as he knew he had no other choice but to perfom well but he was also confident since he had spent the four years burning the mid night oil preparing for this exam.When the exam day reached benson could not believe his eyes.He expected the school to put very strict protocols to prevent them from cheating but that was not the case.People were copying left right and center.The person who was seated infront of him even managed to sneak a text book which had answers to the examroom.The person who was seated next to him on his right side had actually sneaked a phone.He had changed the wallpaper of his phone to that of a picture of a calculator.Actually the teacher who was invigilating them as they did the exam at one point stood next to him but he could not notice that he was using a phone and not a calculator.While everyone was cheating benson had decided to be truthful to himself and he decided he would not cheat in the exams despite the fact that the exam was not easy.As they came out of the examroom everyone was smilling as they all knew that they had a leakage.Fastfoward two months later the exam had been marked and it was now time for the results.The schools had been closed and they were all at home.On that day benson was so anxious he could not sleep or eat anything.The minister of education came out to announce the results.The minister announced the code which they would use to get their results.Benson tried dialling the code to get the results but the results were not showing up.He thought that maybe the system had been overwhelmed since over one million candidates were also dialling the same code at the same time to get their results.He kept on trying again but he was still not getting his results.He made a phone call to the education ministry and the response he got changed his life forever.The unexpected had happened.
