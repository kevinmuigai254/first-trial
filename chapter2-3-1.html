CHAPTER ONE

It was a fine evening that benson had decided to go for a walk.While he was walking along the road he noticed a large convoy heading towards his hood.It was a rare moment to see something like that since he lived in a poor hood where only a few people had cars.It was only after he came back home that he found all those cars lined up outside their house.At first he thought that the president or a top government official had decided to visit them.There was jubilation and celebration everywhere.Benson did not know what was going on.Just as he arrived at the gate he saw the media people running towards him.He was received a warm hug from his mother and family.All this time he did not know what was going on.Everyone was congratulating him.That was when he was told that the minister for education had decided to announce the results for the national exams on that day by surprise.Benson was told that he was among the top perfomers.The media was all over the place as they all wanted to interview him.Benson had to pinch himself to confirm that he was not dreaming and that this was a reality.When he was being interviewed by the press he remembered that his mother was not well off financially and so he asked people of good will to contribute to him so that he would join senior school.To his surprise two days after he had asked people to contribute a fundraiser event was organised.So many people attended the event.All the guests had already arrived on time.Benson could not believe his eyes as hundreds people had turned up for his event,including family and friends and church members.Benson had become suddenly famous.It was a big day for him and his family.There was even a dj and an mc for the event.The fundraiser started and people started contributing the money.Some contributed cash money others did it through their phones.Everything was going on well untill it was time to count the money that had been contributed,the mc of the event announced that the total money that had been contributed was nine hundred thousand dollars.That was when one of the main guests in the event objected and asked how it was possible for the total contribution to be nine thousand dollars while he had contributed ten thousand dollars.The event was now taking a different turn as the hundreds of people in the event who had also contributed demanded that the money should be counted again while everyone was watching.The money was counted again and this time round the total amount was thirty thousand dollars.Just as they were about to start attacking the mc who had initially tried to fool them,the mc took the microphone and he defended himself claiming that he was fooling them since it was actually happy fools day.To his luck the date of that day was actually first april.The mc together with the event planners had planned to take the extra money but to their bad luck they had been caught red handed.Despite that the event still went on.There was a meal prepared for the event.Surprisingly when the contributions had ended and it was time to eat the number of people in the event trippled.Infact those who were at the front of the queue waiting to be served food were the ones who had not contributed anything.Infact those who never contributed were the ones who ate the most food.Just as they thought that the drama had ended a young man went straight to the podium and picked up the microphone from the mc.The young man had heard that this event was going to be a fundraiser and so he also came to ask for help.He took the microphone and he began to narrate his story.He claimed that he was homeless and poor and that he needed the people present to assist him financially.Ironically he claimed that his name was rich.Luckily for him the people present were generous and they contributed to help him get a home and even one of the main guests offered him a job.Despite all the drama it was still a successful day for benson as he now had the money to go back to school.His mother was so delighted.Infact the following day they went for shopping to buy the things that benson needed in school.Benson was so excited to join highshool since he was joining his dream school.It was his first time being in a boarding school.On the first day there was no learning as they went round the school.He was shown his class and also the domitory that he would be sleeping at.Benson also met his new classmates and later on he also met his new cube mates since the dormitories were organised into cubes and each cube was to be occupied by four students.The dormitories were well built but benson was still not comfortable since the bed he was to sleep on was not as luxurious as the bed he slept on at home but he had no other choice but to adapt to the new environment.Well it did not take long before his problems started.In the middle of the night as benson was busy sleeping the unexpected happened.
